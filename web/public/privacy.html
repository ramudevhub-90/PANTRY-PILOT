Privacy policy

Pantry Pilot · last updated 22 October 2026

Pantry Pilot is a kitchen assistant for Alexa+, the web and WhatsApp. It keeps track of the food in your kitchen, suggests meals and helps you shop. This page explains what we store and why.

What we store

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your kitchen data: pantry items, grocery list, meal plans, timers and food preferences (diet, cuisines, city, language).
  • Your WhatsApp number, if you add it, to send order updates and to answer messages you send to Pantry Pilot on WhatsApp.
  • Sign-ins to shopping apps you connect (Swiggy Instamart, Zepto, BigBasket, Kroger), so Pantry Pilot can read orders, compare prices and fill carts when you ask.
  • Photos and bills you send are read by Amazon Nova to list the groceries in them. WhatsApp photos are deleted within a day.

How we use it

Only to run Pantry Pilot for you. Orders are placed only after you hear the items and total read back and say "place the order". We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

On Amazon Web Services (Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon Cognito) in the United States. AI features use Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova). WhatsApp messages go through AWS End User Messaging Social and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Your choices

  • Turn WhatsApp updates off at any time in the app (Shopping apps → WhatsApp → Turn off), or block the number in WhatsApp.
  • Disconnect any shopping app in the app.
  • To delete your account and data, email us and we'll do it within 7 days.
